At 5 mm separation, the plates have a capacitance of approximately 17.7 pF.

Capacitance formula

C = ε₀ εᵣ A / d

C = capacitance · A = overlapping plate area · d = separation · ε₀ = permittivity of free space · εᵣ = relative permittivity.

*The electric field estimate assumes a constant 1 V across the gap. Fringing effects are neglected in the capacitance calculation.

04 / Understand the working

  1. Two conductive plates form a capacitor.
  2. One plate remains fixed while the other moves.
  3. The movement changes the separation, d.
  4. Smaller separation produces greater capacitance.
  5. A measuring circuit can convert this change into an electrical output representing displacement.
Decrease the gap to see capacitance increase.
