3. Working Principle

A variable-distance capacitive transducer measures displacement by detecting a change in capacitance between two plates.

C = ε₀ εᵣ A / d

C = capacitance, ε₀ = permittivity of free space, εᵣ = relative permittivity, A = overlapping plate area, and d = distance between the plates.

When the plate separation decreases, capacitance increases.

4. Applications

  • Displacement measurement
  • Position sensing in automation
  • Precision dimensional measurement
  • Vibration and movement monitoring
